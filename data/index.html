Social Media Sentiment Dashboard

This dashboard presents an analysis of social media posts, focusing on sentiment distribution, geographical trends, and prevalent topics. Utilizing data visualization techniques, we aim to uncover patterns and insights that inform understanding and decision-making.

Reflection

Audience: Anyone interested in social media trends and sentiment (students, marketers, researchers).

Goal: Make complex post data easy to understand: Who posts, what they say, and how they feel.

What worked: Bar chart and map made patterns obvious, word cloud was visually engaging, tooltips added value.
Challenges: Matching country codes, cleaning data, and keeping charts readable.
Compromises: Limited rare words/countries and simplified legends for clarity.

Takeaway: Most viewers understood the dashboard, but some suggested moving the legend, adding filters, or letting users interact with the word cloud.
